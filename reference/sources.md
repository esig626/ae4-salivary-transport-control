# Source priorities and interpretation

Read the original experimental and modelling papers. Do not treat previous
assistant explanations, summary reports or generated prompts as primary data.

1. Pena-Munzenmayer et al. (2015), Ae4 (Slc4a9) Anion Exchanger Drives Cl Uptake-dependent Fluid Secretion by Mouse Submandibular Gland Acinar Cells. DOI: 10.1074/jbc.M114.612895. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4409235/ . Native salivary gland/cell observations, including assay conditions, chloride, pH and secretion. Record whether uncertainty is SEM and whether a measurement is an uptake assay, a resting state, or gland flow. Approximately unchanged pH is not evidence that deletion must cause acidification. Unchanged assay activity is not identical net transport at different ionic gradients.
2. Vera-Siguenza et al. (2018), A Mathematical Model Supports a Key Role for Ae4 (Slc4a9) in Salivary Gland Secretion. DOI: 10.1007/s11538-017-0370-6. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5792321/ . Compare the NHE1 and NKCC1 constitutive laws and water balances. The imported concordance is a secondary audit, not an instruction to copy all legacy equations or their unresolved unit/sign conventions.
3. Hisamitsu et al. (2007), Functional importance of charged residues within the putative intracellular loops in pH regulation by Na+/H+ exchanger NHE1. DOI: 10.1111/j.1742-4658.2007.05962.x. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17662110/ . Evidence for intrinsic intracellular pH regulation. A quantitative curve from another cell type is not a measured salivary NHE1 curve.
4. Pena-Munzenmayer et al. (2016), Ae4 (Slc4a9) is an electroneutral monovalent cation-dependent Cl/HCO3 exchanger. https://rupress.org/jgp/article/147/5/423/43529/Ae4-Slc4a9-is-an-electroneutral-monovalent-cation . Preserve the distinction between cation transport evidence and the model's working 1:1:2 stoichiometry/donor partition.

The ~30 to 35% loss of secretion is a known comparison target, not an unseen
holdout and not a fitting objective in Task 28. A 20% WT AE4 loading scenario
below is a declared exploratory allocation, NOT an experimental measurement,
NOT inferred by subtracting a published NKCC1 knockout flow effect from 100%,
and NOT a promise of a 20% or 30% secretion response.

The previous model algebra suggests a possible repair; it does not establish
that NHE1 pH regulation alone can produce an admissible exact null. Finite
search failure is not proof of global or biological impossibility.
