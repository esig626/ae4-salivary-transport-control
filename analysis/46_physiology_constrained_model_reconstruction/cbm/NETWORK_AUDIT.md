# Single network audit

The matrix has 21 fluxes and 13 recorded rows. Exact rational elimination gives rank 11 and nullity 10. The two charge audit rows are exact Na+K−Cl−TA combinations. All ten cell/lumen amount rows and the separate neutral outflow constraint are retained. No biological row is silently discarded. Five independent worker audits examine this same draft before publication.

Let N, A, E, H and B denote NKCC1, AE4, signed AE2, NHE1 and NBC; P=Pa+Pb, K=Ka+Kb and J is apical chloride export. The exact intracellular constraints are

```
N + H + B − A/2 − 3P = 0
N − A/2 + 2P − K = 0
2N + E + A − J = 0
−E + 2B − 2A + Db + Da = 0
H − E + 2B − 2A = 0
```

Hence 2A=H+2B−E, Db+Da=H, J=6P−H, K=5P−H−B and J=P+K+B. These statements hold only for this explicitly conditional source architecture. In particular the inherited equal AE4 routing is not experimental proof of microscopic stoichiometry. AE4 and NKCC1 columns cannot be swapped while holding every other flux fixed.

All apical cell sources appear with opposite lumen signs. Basolateral and paracellular transfers connect to the named bath reservoir. Lumen bicarbonate outflow removes one TIC and one TA; dissolved CO2 removes one TIC and zero TA. The positive outflow constraint is oNa+oK=oCl+oHCO3. No free intracellular acid or alkalinity drain exists. The lumen equations imply pHCO3=−oHCO3 and Da=−oCO2; thus Db=H+oCO2. Neutral outflow plus lumen balance imposes zero lumen transport current; the cell charge row imposes zero net cell transport current.

NBC imports one Na and two bicarbonates and is electrogenic. Pump cycles export three Na and import two K. Electrogenic columns are audited against their transferred charge, not incorrectly required to have individually zero cell charge. NKCC1, AE4, AE2 and NHE1 are individually electroneutral in the retained coordinates.

The network deliberately does not infer transporter capacity from historical fitted quantities. Unknown finite box bounds use M=100 in both WT and KO, with one predeclared M=1000 comparison. Only A is set to zero in KO. J<=1 is a reference truncation, not physical maximum secretion. Remaining directional choices define the conditional forward loading, proton extrusion, K efflux and secretion sector. AE2, CO2 and paracellular fluxes retain signed bounds. These choices neither establish a common set of electrochemical potentials for every feasible state nor validate resting/stimulated concentrations. The inherited reference directions motivate the sector; no global thermodynamic result is claimed.

The original water times luminal composition closure is relaxed to independent bicarbonate/CO2/cation/chloride outflows. Carbonate and H/OH corrections are neglected explicitly. Consequently this is a sustained flux structural screen, not a quantitative whole gland, water flow or pH model. Outflow components may be zero at endpoints; this is a structural boundary state, not evidence of a physiological composition. ATP demand is exactly P cycles per flux unit; no measured ATP supply cap is known, so energy availability remains an unresolved capacity constraint.
