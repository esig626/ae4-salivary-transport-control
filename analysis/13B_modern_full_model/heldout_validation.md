# Held-out validation disposition

## Decision

The strict AE4-null secretion ledger was **not revealed**.  The replacement
native-source G5 hierarchy did not produce a WT model that passed the absolute
one-SMG flow gate, so the staged-release rule prohibited genotype evaluation.
`results/13B_modern_full_model/reveal_log.json` therefore remains append-only
with `heldout_reveals: []`.

This is not a missing numerical run.  Ten `N_ABS_NKCC` scale-4 roots passed
WT resting ion, pH, positivity, rank, branch, conservation, solver, tolerance,
nearby-state, sustainment, co-stimulation, and regulatory-contract checks.
All ten nevertheless required an observation scale of
`6362.31--8210.72 uL min^-1 per (pL s^-1)`, above the deliberately generous
one-SMG ceiling `3088.15386`.  The complete higher-capacity stress tier then
produced zero additional WT-rest-passing roots.

Consequently no KO/WT secretion ratio, early/late comparison, or knockout
ionic trajectory is scientifically licensed in Task 13B.  The held-out target
cannot be used to rescue an invalid WT denominator.

## Decision-critical measurement

Measure absolute fluid secretion per identified WT acinar secretory unit under
the matched `0.3 uM` carbachol plus `5 uM` isoproterenol protocol for 600 s,
with 10 s resolution, while imaging the number and volume of participating
acinar cells.  Report lumen-volume increase (pL/s per cell) and cumulative
volume, not a normalized fluorescence proxy.

The discriminating threshold is the independently frozen one-SMG requirement:
`0.00291436--0.00323818 pL/s` per modeled `1.30 pL` cell.  A measured WT unit
rate in this band licenses the one-cell observation map and falsifies the
present source-map dynamics; a rate near the modeled native range licenses the
cell equations but falsifies the current one-SMG unit/count map.  Thus the
same experiment selects between two non-knockout-compatible interpretations
that currently differ by more than a factor of two in absolute gland output.

No AE4-null animal or held-out secretion datum is needed for this decision.
