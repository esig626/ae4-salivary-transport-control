# Carbon escape and NHE1 sensitivity of routed AE4 compensation

## Result

The complete fixed 4 by 3 grid was evaluated for all ten retained roots, with separate WT and 5% AE4 resting states and three production calcium levels.  No phenotype value entered model construction, root selection, or parameter choice.

All 240 resting states and all 720 stimulated trajectories passed the inherited numerical and conservation gates.

The interventions did not break routed AE4 self rescue. Across the fixed grid, nonbaseline AE4 retention was 81.614% to 83.758%, and none of the 360 root and calcium comparisons showed lower secretion with 5% AE4.

## Answers to the diagnostic questions

### A. Carbon accumulation

No. Accumulation persisted in every increased CO2 permeability case. With CO2 permeability above baseline, the WT to 5% AE4 TIC change ranged from 1.4992 to 5.7035 mM and the bicarbonate change ranged from 1.4978 to 5.6952 mM. At REST, apical CO2 flux was outward and basolateral CO2 flux was inward in every increased permeability state; the combined CO2 flux remained inward in every state.

### B. Alkalinisation

No. Reduced NHE1 attenuated but did not prevent alkalinisation in any case. With NHE1 below baseline, the pH change ranged from +0.0743 to +0.1693, compared with +0.2008 to +0.2561 in the exact control.

### C. AE4 compensation

The median per expression compensation was 16.279 at baseline, 16.404 for CO2 ×10 alone, 16.703 for NHE1 ×0.25 alone, and 16.711 for the joint intervention. Neither intervention reduced compensation; both increased it slightly relative to the baseline median. Reducing NHE1 to 0.25 lowered median absolute WT AE4 loading from 0.004233 to 0.001631 fmol s⁻¹ and 5% AE4 loading from 0.003444 to 0.001362 fmol s⁻¹. The WT reference fell slightly more in proportion, so the normalised compensation ratio rose despite weaker absolute AE4 flux.

### D. AE4 flux retention

No. Every nonbaseline combination had a higher median retention. The baseline median retention was 81.396%. The lowest grid median was 81.770% at CO2 ×2 and NHE1 ×1. Individual nonbaseline values ranged from 81.614% to 83.758%.

### E. NKCC1 replacement

Yes for every parameter combination by the median replacement criterion. Across individual roots and grid points, the replacement fraction ranged from 0.550 to 0.745, with median 0.672.

### F. Secretion direction

No parameter combination reduced secretion in every root at every calcium.

### G. Mechanistic attribution

No secretion decrease occurred, so that association cannot be assigned.

## Exact regression control

The CO2 ×1, NHE1 ×1 control reproduced Task 24.  The largest normalised resting coordinate difference was 1.169e-13. The largest WT secretion relative error was 2.301e-13, the largest 5% AE4 relative error was 7.700e-14, and the secretion change remained +0.373% to +1.037%.

## Numerical status

Direct REST solution was sufficient for every state.  No resting integration was used. The maximum scaled independent REST residual was 2.739e-11, the maximum current residual was 4.847e-26 A, the maximum REST conservation ratio was 5.347e-03, and the maximum stimulation conservation ratio was 6.052e-03.

Continuation found 0 nonselected admissible root clusters. Any such roots are recorded in `alternative_roots.csv`; selection remained on the branch nearest the declared predecessor.

## Interpretation boundaries

The mathematical result is the response of this fixed model to the declared sensitivity grid. The model mechanism can identify whether changes in carbon, pH, AE4 driving, and NKCC1 loading move together inside these equations. It does not establish that CO2 permeability or NHE1 causes the experimental AE4 phenotype.

The experimental secretion deficit was not read or used until after every simulation and summary was complete. The CO2 and NHE1 factors remain unmeasured sensitivities, and agreement with an experimental percentage would not make a grid point biologically correct.
