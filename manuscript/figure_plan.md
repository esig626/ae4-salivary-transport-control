# Figure-led article plan

The paper should be read as a ten-year reassessment of the mechanism proposed in 2018. Each figure answers one question and sets up the next one.

## Figure 1 - What changed in the model?

**Question:** What has been added since 2018, and why?

The figure contrasts the original concentration model with the reconstructed amount model. It highlights conserved TIC and TA, finite carbonate chemistry, the NBC-like pathway, source-fixed NHE1 and NKCC1, and beta/cAMP/PKA regulation of AE4.

**Story:** We did not simply refit the 2018 model. We rebuilt the bookkeeping that determines whether a proposed mechanism is physically and physiologically possible.

## Figure 2 - Why NBC matters

**Question:** Can AE4 carry a substantial wild-type chloride load while pH remains physiological?

The wild-type positive chloride-loading partition is 79.9% NKCC1, 20.1% AE4 and 0% AE2.

**Story:** NBC does not load chloride. It supplies the bicarbonate and alkalinity that AE4 consumes. The transporter omitted from the original model is structurally necessary once AE4 is asked to do meaningful work.

## Figure 3 - Why equal routing is insufficient

**Question:** Does changing the AE4 sodium/potassium split recover the phenotype?

Task 40 gives only a 3-4% loss, whereas the target-selected Task 41 construction gives 23-30%. The time courses are different: Task 40 gives a small late deficit, while Task 41 gives a large early deficit that declines.

**Story:** Phenotype magnitude and phenotype timing are independent constraints. Neither current model satisfies both.

## Figure 4 - What compensation is doing

**Question:** Why does the reconstructed model resist AE4 loss?

NKCC1 compensation falls from roughly 30% in Task 39 to 21-23% in Task 40 and 3-6% in Task 41.

**Story:** A missing AE4 flux is not a secretion deficit. The network decides whether the loss matters.

## Figure 5 - Do the Catalán 2025 mechanism classes solve it?

**Question:** Can updated AE4 stoichiometry recover the phenotype without the artificial CaCC dependency?

No. C1 predicts more secretion after deletion, C3a predicts almost no phenotype, C3b gives only 7.7% and is thermodynamically opposed, and all carbonate classes fail stimulated wild-type pH.

**Story:** The Catalán experiments constrain AE4 at the molecular scale, but stoichiometric source replacement alone is insufficient at the whole-cell scale. pH is a mechanism discriminator.

## Figure 6 - What survives ten years later?

**Question:** Which conclusions remain?

The original model produced 24%, Task 40 produces 3.9%, Task 42 at most 7.7%, and the target-selected construction produces 30.3% against the experimental 35 +/- 4.7%.

**Story:** The biological importance of AE4 survives. The original network explanation does not. The remaining problem is sustained regulation, not simply ion stoichiometry.
