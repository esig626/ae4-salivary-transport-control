# Claims and evidence ledger

## Directly established by the reconstructed model

1. The conserved acid-base architecture requires an additional inward alkalinity source if AE4 is to carry a substantial stimulated chloride load while pH remains physiological.
2. The NBC-like structural pathway gives a valid 600 s wild-type trajectory with 79.9248% NKCC1, 20.0752% AE4 and 0% AE2 positive chloride loading.
3. Equal AE4 sodium/potassium routing gives only 3.4176% and 3.8575% cumulative secretion loss at 5% and zero AE4.
4. Under equal routing, the explicit AE4 term cancels from the stationary apical chloride balance, leaving `J_CaCC = 6 J_pump - J_NHE1`.
5. None of the seven predeclared Catalán 2025 source classes recovers the held-out secretion phenotype under the inherited Task 40 scalar AE4 kinetic law.
6. C2, C4a and C4b fail the stimulated wild-type pH gate despite passing the resting-state gate.
7. A target-selected AE4-dependent CaCC recruitment law can produce 23.16% and 30.26% secretion losses while passing the declared 600 s gates.

## Historical constraints recovered from the corrected 2018 source

1. The 2018 model predicted an approximately 24% reduction in secretion after AE4 deletion, not the full experimental loss.
2. The 2018 model reached the reduced AE4-null flow in about 2 min, whereas the experimental divergence developed over roughly 10 min.
3. The 2018 model predicted only minimal NKCC1 activation after AE2 or AE4 deletion.
4. The 2018 acid-base architecture included CO2 hydration, NHE1, AE2 and AE4, but no other proton or bicarbonate transport mechanism.
5. The 2018 lumen was held at constant volume, so total luminal water inflow equalled outflow. The reconstructed model instead evolves a finite lumen explicitly.

## Experimental findings taken from the literature

1. AE4 deletion reduces mouse submandibular gland secretion by approximately 35 +/- 4.7% and slows chloride reuptake.
2. AE2 deletion has little or no effect on secretion.
3. In the isolated 2015 assay, NKCC1-dependent chloride uptake was not detectably different between control and AE4- or AE2-deficient acinar cells.
4. The same study found no genotype-dependent change in stimulation-induced NHE-dependent alkalinisation or resting intracellular pH.
5. AE4 is an electroneutral monovalent-cation-dependent chloride/bicarbonate exchanger.
6. Beta-adrenergic/cAMP/PKA signalling activates AE4.
7. Catalán et al. 2025 experimentally distinguish sodium- and potassium-supported transport and propose, but do not prove, several whole-cycle stoichiometries.

## Model-dependent interpretations

1. NBC is structurally necessary in this model, but the implementation is not an isoform assignment or a measured abundance.
2. Strong NKCC1 compensation is the principal reason the Task 40 phenotype is small.
3. The Task 40 NKCC1 compensation is an integrated model response during the 600 s combined stimulus. It is not the same observable or protocol as the isolated 2015 NKCC1 assay and should be presented as an experimentally unresolved tension.
4. The Task 42 negative result applies to source-vector substitution under inherited kinetics and does not falsify the Catalán molecular mechanisms.
5. Task 41 localises one class of missing coupling but does not validate AE4-dependent CaCC regulation.
6. The model represents primary acinar secretion. Ductal modification of final saliva is outside its scope even though whole-gland flow is used as the phenotype benchmark.

## Claims that must not be made

1. Catalán et al. 2025 proved any of the Task 42 whole-cycle stoichiometries.
2. Task 42 disproves Catalán et al. 2025.
3. Task 41 independently predicts the knockout phenotype.
4. The NBC capacity is measured or identifies a particular acinar NBC isoform.
5. Strong NKCC1 compensation after AE4 loss is experimentally established.
6. The model reproduces final duct-modified saliva composition.
7. Passing the 600 s gates establishes long-time physiological validity.
