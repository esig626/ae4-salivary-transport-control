# Claims and evidence ledger

## Directly established by the reconstructed model

1. The conserved acid-base architecture requires an additional inward alkalinity source if AE4 is to carry a substantial stimulated chloride load while pH remains physiological.
2. The NBC-like structural pathway gives a valid 600 s wild-type trajectory with 79.9248% NKCC1, 20.0752% AE4 and 0% AE2 positive chloride loading.
3. Equal AE4 sodium/potassium routing gives only 3.4176% and 3.8575% cumulative secretion loss at 5% and zero AE4.
4. Under equal routing, the explicit AE4 term cancels from the stationary apical chloride balance, leaving `J_CaCC = 6 J_pump - J_NHE1`.
5. None of the seven predeclared Catalán 2025 source classes recovers the held-out secretion phenotype under the inherited Task 40 scalar AE4 kinetic law.
6. C2, C4a and C4b fail the stimulated wild-type pH gate despite passing the resting-state gate.
7. A target-selected AE4-dependent CaCC recruitment law can produce 23.16% and 30.26% secretion losses while passing the declared 600 s gates.

## Experimental findings taken from the literature

1. AE4 deletion reduces mouse submandibular gland secretion by approximately 35 +/- 4.7% and slows chloride reuptake.
2. AE2 deletion has little or no effect on secretion.
3. AE4 is an electroneutral monovalent-cation-dependent chloride/bicarbonate exchanger.
4. Beta-adrenergic/cAMP/PKA signalling activates AE4.
5. Catalán et al. 2025 experimentally distinguish sodium- and potassium-supported transport and propose, but do not prove, several whole-cycle stoichiometries.

## Model-dependent interpretations

1. NBC is structurally necessary in this model, but the implementation is not an isoform assignment or a measured abundance.
2. Strong NKCC1 compensation is the principal reason the Task 40 phenotype is small.
3. The Task 42 negative result applies to source-vector substitution under inherited kinetics and does not falsify the Catalán molecular mechanisms.
4. Task 41 localises one class of missing coupling but does not validate AE4-dependent CaCC regulation.

## Claims that must not be made

1. Catalán et al. 2025 proved any of the Task 42 whole-cycle stoichiometries.
2. Task 42 disproves Catalán et al. 2025.
3. Task 41 independently predicts the knockout phenotype.
4. The NBC capacity is measured or identifies a particular acinar NBC isoform.
5. Passing the 600 s gates establishes long-time physiological validity.
