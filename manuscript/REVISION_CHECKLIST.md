# Revision checklist before journal submission

## Highest priority scientific checks

- Confirm the exact experimental AE4-null secretion reduction and uncertainty used in the text. Current wording uses “approximately 35%”.
- Decide whether the primary comparator is total 600 s secretion, endpoint flow, or the experimental early/late time course.
- Add a publication-quality table containing every frozen model parameter and its provenance.
- Audit all equations in `main.tex` against the production implementation, including current signs and apical/basolateral pump partitions.
- Decide whether the generic pump and channel formulae should remain in the main text or move to an appendix.
- Add explicit values for solver tolerances and every physiological gate to a Methods table.
- Confirm that the Task 42 affinity sign convention is described consistently in the manuscript and source contract.
- Decide how strongly to frame the NBC pathway: structural surrogate, candidate isoform, or generic inward bicarbonate supply.

## Figures

- Redraw Figure 1 after the author decides the preferred visual language.
- Confirm whether Figure 2 should use Task 37 or Task 40 WT output throughout; the present draft combines the Task 40 time course with the Task 37 integrated partition because both describe the same reconstructed architecture.
- Add experimental time-course points to Figure 5 if digitisation or original data are available.
- Decide whether Task 41 belongs in the main paper or Supplementary Material after the Task 42 discussion is finalised.
- Generate vector-only journal figures with final fonts and panel dimensions.

## Writing

- Confirm title and whether “nearly a decade later” or “a decade later” is appropriate at submission.
- Confirm author list, order, affiliations, corresponding author and ORCIDs.
- Add a short paragraph explaining why NBC was omitted in 2018 if the original modelling notes can establish the reason. Do not invent one from memory.
- Tighten the Introduction after all authors agree on how much of the ten-year history belongs in the main text.
- Add a concise comparison with other SLC4 transporter models and pH-control models.
- Check terminology: AE4/Ae4 and SLC4A9/Slc4a9 should follow species and journal conventions consistently.

## Submission preparation

- Convert to the current Springer Nature `sn-jnl` template.
- Flatten figures and bibliography files for Editorial Manager.
- Add funding, acknowledgements, author contributions, data availability and competing-interest statements.
- Compile with the journal-supported TeX Live version and inspect the final PDF page by page.
- Create a clean submission ZIP distinct from the research repository.
