# Task 21: pooled AE4 WT physiology recalibration

## Goal

Rebuild the WT calibration around the Task 20 pooled-cation, no-slip AE4 mechanism without forcing the new mechanism to preserve the exact internal WT state generated by the legacy independent-Na/K-branch model.

The scientific question is:

> Can the pooled-cation no-slip AE4 mechanism support a physiologically admissible WT salivary-acinar steady state and, once calibrated using WT evidence only, does reducing AE4 expression to 5% reduce secretion while AE2 loss remains comparatively neutral?

This is a WT-first calibration and held-out genotype test. The AE4-loss and AE2-loss outcomes must not influence WT calibration, state selection, parameter selection, bounds, ranking, or root retention.

## Repository inheritance

Start from merged Task 20 on `main`.

Use the Task 20 `POOLED_CATION_112_NO_SLIP` AE4 implementation unchanged as the primary AE4 mechanism.

Do not reintroduce independent Na/K branches or any Na/K slip loop.

Do not change the 1:1:2 Cl:cation:HCO3 working stoichiometry in Task 21. Alternative stoichiometries are a separate future question.

Do not edit `archive/`.

## Why the exact inherited WT state is no longer fixed

Task 20 showed that every legacy inherited WT state has negative pooled AE4 affinity under the pooled 1:1:2 no-slip law, while productive WT AE4 requires positive chloride loading. That contradiction is structural at those old states.

Those exact states were themselves generated under the legacy AE4 architecture. Therefore, in Task 21 they are numerical starting points and provenance references only. They are not biological observations and are not hard calibration targets.

Allow the WT conserved state to move and re-equilibrate under the new AE4 law.

## Stage 0: predeclare the WT physiology contract before optimisation

Before running the new WT optimisation, create a machine-readable contract in:

`results/21_pooled_ae4_wt_physiology/precalibration_contract.json`

and a short human-readable source note in:

`analysis/21_pooled_ae4_wt_physiology/wt_physiology_contract.md`

The contract must be committed and pushed before any Task 21 WT optimisation results are inspected.

The following measured WT targets are fixed from the existing evidence ledger:

- resting intracellular chloride: `50.10 ± 1.50 mM`;
- resting intracellular pH: `6.91 ± 0.07`.

Use predeclared acceptance bands equal to ±2 reported SEM:

- chloride: `47.10 <= [Cl-]i <= 53.10 mM`;
- pH: `6.77 <= pHi <= 7.05`.

These are modelling screening bands. Do not describe them as population confidence intervals.

Also predeclare broad physiological envelopes for:

- intracellular Na;
- intracellular K;
- cell volume.

Derive those numerical envelopes from the repository's existing provenance/source ledger and established physiological references already used by the project. Prefer broad biologically plausible bounds over narrow inherited-model values. Record the exact source and rationale for each bound.

If no direct native-submandibular measurement exists for one of these quantities, say so explicitly and use a broad physiology/provenance envelope. Do not fabricate a measurement.

The legacy WT Na, K and volume values may be used as starting points or provenance references but must not be converted into narrow hard targets merely because the old model used them.

Do not use any AE4-loss secretion result, AE2-loss result, Task 14B phenotype, Task 18 phenotype, Task 19 phenotype, or the approximately 35% experimental AE4-loss deficit when setting these bounds.

## WT hard constraints

A Task 21 WT candidate is admissible only if all of the following hold:

1. the complete production resting RHS satisfies the inherited numerical steady-state tolerance;
2. membrane-current closure satisfies the inherited numerical tolerance;
3. all Na, K, Cl, TIC, alkalinity, water and lumen bookkeeping/conservation identities pass;
4. all state quantities are finite and physically valid;
5. all fitted capacities/permeabilities are finite and nonnegative;
6. all fractions remain in `[0,1]`;
7. resting chloride lies in `[47.10, 53.10] mM`;
8. resting pH lies in `[6.77, 7.05]`;
9. resting Na, K and cell volume lie inside the predeclared Stage-0 physiological envelopes;
10. pooled AE4 has positive thermodynamic affinity for the declared positive chloride-loading cycle at resting WT;
11. pooled AE4 net chloride flux is strictly positive at resting WT;
12. the pooled no-slip invariant holds: Na and K AE4 transported amounts follow the same scalar cycle direction, with no independent opposing Na/K cycle;
13. production dynamics from that calibrated WT state are numerically valid at calcium `0.10`, `0.25`, and `0.50 uM`.

Do not impose a 10% or 30% AE4 chloride-loading share in Task 21.

Do not force the new pooled AE4 chloride flux to equal the legacy AE4 flux.

AE4's WT capacity and productive chloride contribution must emerge from the WT-only calibration.

## WT calibration objective

Use WT information only.

For every physiologically and physically admissible candidate, rank solutions lexicographically:

1. minimise the normalized squared deviation of the measured WT observables from their reported means:
   - `([Cl-]i - 50.10)/1.50`;
   - `(pHi - 6.91)/0.07`;
2. subject to that WT-observable fit, minimise the largest absolute log-fold change among adjustable positive capacities relative to their inherited reference values;
3. subject to 1 and 2, minimise the sum of squared log-fold changes of those adjustable positive capacities.

Do not include any genotype output in the objective.

Do not penalise state coordinates for departing from their legacy WT values beyond the declared physiological constraints. The legacy state is not data.

## Ensemble/root discipline

Use all ten inherited WT roots as independent numerical seeds for the new WT calibration. Additional solver starts are allowed only for numerical robustness, not for phenotype-guided searching.

Do not assume each legacy seed must converge to a distinct new WT state.

Deduplicate genuinely identical calibrated solutions using a documented numerical criterion.

Retain every distinct admissible calibrated WT solution that passes the same WT-only acceptance rule. Do not discard a valid solution because its later AE4-loss phenotype is inconvenient.

A local optimiser failure is not proof of structural infeasibility. Distinguish solver nonconvergence from a proven physical/thermodynamic contradiction.
